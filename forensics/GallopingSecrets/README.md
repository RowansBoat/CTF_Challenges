### Name

Galloping Secrets

### Description

```
A sacred scroll has been delivered from a mystery city's Lunar New Year festival. Find the city, then determine the year of the city's first ever Year of the Horse festival and how many times they have celebrated the Lunar New Year Festival altogether (not just year of the horse). Add the two numbers together for your flag.
Flag format: CTF{XXXX}
```

### Difficulty

Medium

### Flag
<details>`CTF{2035}`</details>

### Solution
<details>
1. Find the coordinates hidden in scroll.jpg using Forensically ELA or similar tool
2. Coordinates point to San Francisco, CA
3. Research reveals the SF Chinese New Year Parade began under the Chamber of Commerce in 1958
4. The first Year of the Horse since 1958 = 1966
4. 2026 is the 69th annual parade (1958 being the 1st)
5. 1966 + 69 = CTF{2035}
</details>
