### Name

Whats in the applecore?

### Description

```
The orchard looks calm, but sometimes it’s worth listening closer to what a video has to say…
```

### Difficulty

Hard

### Solution
<details>1. First realize this challenge has nothing to do with the video and all to do with the audio.
2. At around the 12 second mark a weird hum / beeping noise is introduced into the video
3. Take the audio from this audio and look at it in audacity
4. Switch the audio view to spectrogram where you will see the letters UXAH3MBBX3E
5. Realize this is a caeser cipher at shift of 12 using a website like https://cryptii.com/pipes/caesar-cipher</details>
